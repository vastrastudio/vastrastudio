Since 2016

A small shop that grew out of one tailoring table

Vastra Studio started as a single tailoring table in the lanes near Bapu Bazaar, stitching kurtas for neighbours who wanted something better than what the big stores sold. Since then, we still cut and finish everything within Jaipur, working with the same block-print and bandhani karigars we started with.

We offer customization on most of our pieces — size, colour, your name or text, and your own logo or design where it can be applied. Every order is still a conversation: you tell us what you want on WhatsApp, and a real person on our floor confirms the details before it's stitched or packed.
